Questions

Good to know

Is DayHive free?
Every new account gets 7 days free, with no card and no automatic charge. After that you choose monthly, yearly or lifetime access. Account, export and deletion stay available either way.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything you write is saved on your device first. Syncing to your account happens when you are online, and only after you agree to it.
Where is my data?
On your device, and in your private cloud account if you agree to sync. We do not sell your data or show ads. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.
Is there an iPhone version?
It is on the way. Leave your e-mail below and we will send you one message when it is ready.
Is the period calendar medical advice?
No. It gives estimates only. It is not a medical device and must not be used for birth control. Ask a healthcare professional for medical advice.
How do I delete my account?
In the app, open Account and choose Delete account. You can also follow the steps on the delete-account page.

Using an iPhone?

DayHive is coming to the iPhone. Leave your e-mail and we will send you one message when it is ready. Nothing else.

We use your e-mail only to tell you when DayHive is on the iPhone, then delete it. See the Privacy Policy.
