Privacy Policy

DayHive (the "app"). Last updated: 19 October 2026.

DayHive is a personal organiser: habits, money, tasks, a journal, prayer times, English learning, sketches and a teacher's timetable. This page says what the app keeps, where it goes, and what you can do about it. We wrote it to be read.

1. What stays on your device

Everything you write in the app is stored first in a database on your own device: habits, transactions, debts, budgets, tasks, journal entries (with their pictures, voice notes and files), sketches, timetable, settings, and the receipt photos you attach. The configured app requires an account. Before tracker sync starts, we explain cloud use and ask you to agree. If you decline, tracker sync remains off and you can sign out, export existing data or request deletion. Account authentication itself sends the information you enter to our sign-in provider.

3. Who can see it

Your account is intended to restrict access to your records using ownership-based database policies. Authorised service providers process the data to operate the app. We may access information when necessary to investigate a support request, security issue or abuse report. The files in the cloud storage are in a private folder named after your user id. If you use the "together" feature, the habits you choose to share are visible to the partner you accepted, and only those.

4. Services the app talks to

  • Supabase (sign-in, database, file storage): hosts your account and synced records.
  • Resend (e-mail delivery): sends the account e-mails you ask for, such as the sign-up confirmation and password reset. Your e-mail address and the message are sent to it for that purpose only.
  • RevenueCat, Apple App Store and Google Play: subscriptions and purchases.
  • City search: the current app searches its bundled city list locally. Device location can also be requested for prayer calculations. There is no external geocoding request in the default city-search path.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons: the Learn section asks for a picture for a word; the word is sent.
  • Free Dictionary API (api.dictionaryapi.dev): words requested in Learn are sent to obtain definitions, examples and pronunciation. Downloading pictures or pronunciation also contacts the hosting provider.
  • External providers receive normal connection metadata such as IP address and request details. These requests may be necessary when you choose the corresponding online feature.

The app has no advertising and does not sell your data or use it for advertising. When purchases are configured, RevenueCat processes purchase history, account-linked identifiers and subscription information for app functionality and subscription analytics. Review each provider’s privacy terms for its processing.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications and exact alarms: habit, task and prayer reminders. Android asks separately for precise alarm access; if it is refused, reminders may arrive late.
  • Approximate location: only when you ask for prayer times for where you are. Calculations use the coordinates on your device. Saved prayer coordinates and place names can also be included in enabled prayer-settings sync and full cloud backups. No background location tracking is used.
  • Microphone: only when you record a voice note in the journal.
  • Camera and pictures: only for pictures you choose to add (the system picker shows only what you pick).
  • Fingerprint / face: only to unlock the app or a locked entry, handled by the system.

6. Your choices and your data

  • Export: Settings > Backup > Export everything gives you all your data as readable JSON plus your pictures and files.
  • Delete: Account > Delete account requests deletion of your account, records, cloud files, backups and partnership details. Interrupted cleanup is retried; if the request cannot be completed in the app, use the email route. See how to delete your account.
  • Backups: automatic backups on your device are in the app's own folder and are not copied by Android's automatic backup.
  • All tracker records sync after cloud consent. Settings > What to sync controls settings groups; it does not opt individual tracker records out of cloud storage. Scheduled cloud backups include all tracker records and settings, except device identity, the app passcode and device-only security/consent settings. A backup is separate from settings sync. You may stop cloud use by signing out.

Drawings: Sketch drawings currently stay on the device and are visible across accounts using that device. Automatic Sketch cloud uploads are paused. Older cloud copies remain until account deletion. Local exports include device-wide drawings, plus receipts and journal files referenced by the exporting account; unassigned files are kept on the device rather than guessed into another account.

7. How long we keep it

Records remain while your account exists. Deleted journal entries and tasks normally stay in trash for 30 days. Permanently removed attachment files are queued for remote cleanup; old cloud backups can retain an earlier version until the next backup replaces it or account deletion removes it. Account deletion includes stored backups. Abuse reports may be retained only as necessary to resolve the report and comply with legal obligations; account identifiers are detached on account deletion. Google, Apple and payment providers may retain transaction records under their own legal obligations and policies.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Journal PINs are kept as slow salted hashes and these hashes can accompany synced entries and backups. PIN locks hide content in the app; they do not provide end-to-end encryption. The app-wide passcode and device identity are excluded from new cloud backups; previously created backups are replaced on the next backup or removed with account deletion. Please keep your password and recovery codes safe.
