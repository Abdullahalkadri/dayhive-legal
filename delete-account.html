Delete your DayHive account

Last updated: 17 October 2026.

In the app (instant)

  1. Open DayHive and sign in.
  2. Open the side menu and tap your account (Account).
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted at once, together with your profile, your synced records (habits, money, tasks, journal, sketches, timetable, settings), your files and backups in the cloud, your shared habits and your device list.

What stays

Data kept only on your own device (the app's local database) is not on our servers. It is removed when you uninstall the app or use Settings > Delete data. Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google: deleting the account does not cancel a subscription, so cancel it in your store account settings too.
